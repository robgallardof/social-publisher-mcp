# Content strategy

## Pillars
3-4 pillars from `brand/voice.md` (e.g. humor / useful / behind the scenes / community). Each week: every pillar at
least once, none more than half of the week. Tag pillar in the strategist's plan table.

## Cadence
- Consistency beats volume. Start with what the queue can sustain for 4+ weeks (e.g. 1/day, or 3/day if the
  backlog is large). More than ~3/day on one account usually splits reach; test before scaling.
- Every video of a day goes out together at 05:00 (owner's rule). With 2-3 per day consider `stagger_minutes` to
  avoid competing with yourself in the first hour; propose it as a test, do not change it alone.
- 05:00 local: the post is fresh when the audience wakes up; it is a hypothesis like any other: read the
  "best weekdays" and the dashboards' "followers active" hours monthly with `audience-analytics`.

## Sequencing a week
- Strongest video on the best day (from `insights_report`); weakest never on two consecutive days.
- Series: same day/time each week, the "Part 2" only after Part 1 has been live ≥ 24 h.
- Do not publish near-duplicates the same week across one platform.

## Cross-posting
Same video to all four is fine **if** it carries no other platform's watermark (Instagram/YouTube demote TikTok logos):
export clean from the editor. Copy is adapted per platform (`platform-copywriting`). Facebook Reels > 90 s will
post as normal video: trim a version or remove facebook from its `platforms`.

## Experiments
One variable per week (hook type, length band 15-30/30-60/60+, caption style, stagger, time). Write the hypothesis
in the plan; the analyst reads it after ≥ 7 days with ≥ 5 videos per arm. Keep what wins in `brand/`.
