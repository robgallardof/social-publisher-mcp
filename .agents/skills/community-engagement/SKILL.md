# Community engagement

The first hour after 05:00 is mostly asleep; the useful window is the morning and the evening of publish day.

## Routine (publish day, ~08:00 and ~20:00 local)
1. Open each new post (browser tools, see `agent-browser-upload`). Read comments newest first.
2. Reply to: genuine questions (answer briefly, or "lo explico en un video 👀" and note the idea in
   `analytics/research/comment-ideas.md`), early commenters, creators in the niche. Voice from `brand/voice.md`.
3. Pin one comment that adds value (answer to the common question, or the series link).
4. Hide/report spam and scams; do not engage trolls. Never delete criticism that is legitimate; answer it calmly once.

## Approval rules
- Draft replies and show them to the user first unless they enabled "auto-reply" in `brand/voice.md`.
- Never reply about prices, medical/legal topics, refunds, partnerships or anything sensitive: escalate to the user.
- DMs: triage only (business / fan / spam) and summarise; do not answer DMs without approval.
- No fake engagement: no buying, no pods, no replying from other accounts.

## Moderation setup (one time, in each app)
Hidden words / comment filters with the list in `brand/voice.md` (insults, scam phrases, competitor spam links).
